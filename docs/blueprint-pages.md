# Blueprint pages 2–18: the storyboards

Written on 2026-11-03, after the owner accepted the level of the reworked home page (`docs/blueprint-home.md`) and asked for it on every page. Each page below is listed with its job, its opening loop and its scenes in order.

## The rules every page keeps

These come from the owner's review (`docs/blueprint-review.md`).
- **Opening.** Every page opens with a short silent loop:
  - cut from footage of the place where it exists;
  - otherwise made from the brand photograph with a slow camera breath (`scripts/blueprint-media/loops-pages.sh`).
- **Pictures on every screen.** No screen is text alone. The statement block is retired. The facts, notes, table and events blocks carry a picture beside the words.
- **Pictures share one shape.** Every picture is a rectangle rounded from one scale. A group shares one proportion: doors, galleries, steps and facts pictures are 4:5; wide doors and splits are 3:2.
- **Grounds.** A page changes ground at most once, at a clean edge. Where a page goes from light to dark, the nightfall arch carries the change.
- **Numbers** appear only where the content is a real order: the steps of the Hearth and of an evening for two.
- **The close.** Every page ends on the next page's opening, then the footer with the night Dome.
